# AI Disclosure

- KirbyAM Archipelago does **not** contain AI art.
- AI Agents and LLMs do assist with coding and developer documentation, but everything is manually reviewed by the developer:
  - I design all features. That doesn't mean the design is good. I'm not a professional developer. I'm a computer science graduate who dabbles.
  - I use AI for "first drafts" of some minor features. For example, expanding the catalog of health granting items.
  - I use AI for rote processes. For example, creating PRs and running tests.
  - I have used it to trace how data moves through the decompilation (https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam). It's faster for these kinds of things.
- *Everything we put out is manually tested by a human (usually @BlameMateo, sometimes @hasherwi).  We both have to agree something is of good enough quality before it is released.*

## So why do I use these tools?

Because they're tools. In some circumstances, they enable me to go faster. I use my judgement from over a decade of working in technical roles and my education to decide when a tool is useful and when it is not. **Bottom line:** It would have taken months longer, if ever, for this APworld to happen without these tools.

## Do you have wrong opinions against me using AI?

That's great! As a society, we should have these debates. This is a free project I do in my own, very limited, personal time. No one compensates me for this. No one SHOULD compensate me for this. I welcome you to get involved. If you're better at software development and have the time, take it over the project and rewrite it all to be better! That would be awesome!

Still have objections? You don't have to use our project. There are many *better* APworlds out there. I had an idea. No one was working on it. I made some progress.

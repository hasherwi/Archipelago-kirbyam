# Source-backed starting-permission release review

## Provenance

This candidate integrates the reconstructed work based on the verified published
177d765 source tree (`2f7b22b9ba175877db29aaffa6c665b90827880c`) onto the current
056bd931 tree (`aa5cb4b75251652901ed1229d1194a5672cbf3b5`). The latter tree was
verified byte-for-byte before integration. The unavailable older local release
commit and its historical test counts are not used as validation.

## Production scope

- Preserve all 108 original witnesses, including logical subregions, with strict
  raw and 216 compiled Area Key variant fingerprints.
- Add six witnesses: guarded permanent Burning alternatives for 176 and 557,
  and permanent Ice alternatives for 825. These OR with the original finite
  sources; permission ownership alone never substitutes for physical equipment.
- Classify required Ice progression before Main creates regions.
- Preserve the current branch's removal of the unvalidated 742 upper-return
  Warp alias, its lower Burning route, and its counterfactual self-lock test.
- Automatically precollect only Beam. Stone and Burning become progression
  permissions; gating enables 24 pooled ability items. Explicit starting inventory
  remains additive. Historical receipt names and the 22-ability fallback stay frozen.
- Keep the final all-check guard, source-policy validation, bounded solver limits,
  source-assignment budgets, and selected accessibility setting unchanged.
- Preserve the current supported Beam-containing source pool. Arbitrarily narrowed
  custom pools remain unsupported; see `supported-source-monotonicity.md`.

## Native coverage and evidence boundaries

The complete F5 census covers 358 tiles, 81 clusters, and 33 rooms. All clusters
classified as mandatory AP exits or targets have source or mechanism witnesses;
21 bypass clusters have directed Normal coverage. The remaining 25 scenery and
21 optional non-AP pickup clusters stay pending and do not create providers.
This is a classification and evidence review, not an assertion that every metal
tile must be destroyed or every optional room has a controller replay.

The environmental manifest binds ten original records byte-for-byte. Room 502
has separate actual 524 and 617 approaches. Room 510 has its Stone peg/chest proof
and separate Normal exits. Room 532 has fresh-bank directional cannon/Bomb proofs.
Room 736 has separate NW and South/Mini branches; 737 has no return door to 736.
Regression checks preserve those directions, intact-metal observations, exact
check flags, source consumption, and default/500% enemy-health audits.

The goal source, imported carrier, and boss/projectile suffix records can have
different RNG lineages and actor-startup fixtures. Independent review found no
logical incompatibility in their matching room/retained-equipment boundaries.
No damage budget, persistent opening, reused finite ammunition, or acquired
permission is silently transferred between those fixtures. They are bounded
compositional witnesses, not concatenated full-game, direct AP one-HP, universal
multiplayer, server-acknowledgement, or battery-persistence demonstrations.

Raw goal source/carrier/suffix payloads are not bundled. Local review verified
all 16 preserved inputs against the checked-in hash/size references. The 825
scope addendum accepts its suffix only; that original qualification is preserved.
The source and carrier are supported separately, rather than relabeling the
original record as an uninterrupted overall replay.

## Verification

Fresh checks must cover source/access negatives, finite-object consumption,
Main-order inventory, exact masks, all-check closure, frozen-reference planner
comparisons, and native-C ability rerolls. Generation audits decode actual
archives and sweep placed progression across players; granting the entire item
pool is not an accepted substitute. Unsafe minimal fills must fail before archive
output and are counted as refusals, not successful generations.

The historical reconstructed 2,304-case product has matching runtime source/data,
but remains separate from fresh integration validation. It is one defined product
at one seed, not exhaustive coverage of every goal, health setting, or seed.
This remains a draft pull-request candidate, not a merged or released build.

## Method and limits

[Native evidence manifest](wikirby-unavailable-room-native-evidence.json) contains every target identity/AP key, exact F1 coordinates and replacement records, short axial collision samples, object identities, tile transitions and incoming transitions. No F1 replacement is introduced by any decoded mutation record in the 13 rooms. Four-neighbor F1/F2/F3 components are geometric summaries, not a simulation of chain timing or a guarantee of linked effects.

Object streams use **RoomProps +0x20**, then the object-list header's +0 pointer, advancing by each record's +1 size byte until zero, matching [CreateLevelObjects](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_080023A4.c#L5872-L5929). The header's +0x0C is a distinct-type count, not an instance count. This correction also exposed omissions in the separate switch inventory: its corrected total is **35 switches in 24 native rooms**, not 23 in 19. The separate corrected artifacts preserve their earlier audit copies.

[Collision attributes](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_080023A4.c#L71-L104), [mutation replacement semantics](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_080023A4.c#L586-L668), and [RoomProps/ObjectTemplate layouts](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/include/data.h) support the scalar decode. Collision type alone does not prove a viable player stance, entry-side reachability, attack compatibility, item/source availability, dynamic timing or gameplay acceptance. Those remain explicit gaps.

## Olive Ocean, Room 20: native 802

[Room 20, revision 459289](https://wikirby.com/w/index.php?title=Olive_Ocean_-_Room_20&oldid=459289)
describes a one-way route from Room 19 to Chest 2. A required Bomb barrier turns
walls into spikes; later cannons provide access to optional supplies. The room
has no treasure chest. This supports distinguishing the Bomb/cannon environment
from a blanket copy-ability requirement. Native tile and mutation evidence still
determines which particular cells and paths are affected.

## Olive Ocean, Room 21: native 819

[Room 21, revision 459175](https://wikirby.com/w/index.php?title=Olive_Ocean_-_Room_21&oldid=459175)
describes an underwater route from Chest 2 to Room 22. Metal protects a loose
1-Up and other supplies; keeping the previous room's Hammer helps collect them.
The page lists no treasure chest. Its vanilla Hammer advice does not guarantee
that a randomized Bonkers grants Hammer, or make those loose pickups AP checks.

## Olive Ocean, Room 22: native 821

[Room 22, revision 459255](https://wikirby.com/w/index.php?title=Olive_Ocean_-_Room_22&oldid=459255)
links arrivals from Rooms 17 and 21 to a one-way exit into Room 23. It describes
water passages, dry sections, and another loose 1-Up behind metal that can be
reached with retained Hammer. No treasure chest is listed. This corroborates
the inventory's optional-pickup distinction without asserting every underwater
attack direction or a guaranteed randomized source.

## Radish Ruins, Room 2: native 621

[Room 2, revision 459512](https://wikirby.com/w/index.php?title=Radish_Ruins_-_Room_2&oldid=459512)
describes a one-way passage from Room 1 to Room 3, crumbling stone over spikes,
a released Gordo, and a Bomb-triggered water current. No treasure chest is
listed. The crumbling platforms corroborate the native object-overlay finding:
this room's F7 environmental tiles must not be mislabeled reinforced metal.

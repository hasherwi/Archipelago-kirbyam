# Kirby Amazing Mirror collision-avoiding attack ranges, target-specific correction

## Scope and verification

Read-only investigation of `jiangzhengwenjz/katam` at commit `7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189`. The authorized USA ROM was read only to decode animation hitbox records; its SHA-1 was verified as `274b102b6d940f46861a92b4e65f89a51815c12c`. The numeric limits in this report come from source/animation decoding, not emulator distance measurements, and the source checkout was not modified. A separate Rainbow Route Room 8 Spark runtime witness is documented in the inventory; it does not turn these envelopes into demonstrated maximum-distance switch activations.

Distances below are source coordinate envelopes from Kirby's origin for a stationary launch, unless stated otherwise. They are not wall thicknesses or center-to-center target distances. Use the Bomb Block scan column for tiles, and the object-overlap column only after checking small-switch eligibility. One terrain tile is 16 pixels. A sweep's bounding envelope does not mean it fills every point inside that rectangle. Movement, launch height, terrain layout, target eligibility, camera position, object collision, and cancellation can reduce practical reach.

## Why the Bomb Block and small-switch columns differ

The global interaction pass calls `sub_08036CBC` only when the attack's **flags** include 0x80000000. This is separate from 0x100 (skip projectile terrain response) and from an identically numbered bit in **unk68**. The scanner reads `ObjectSetBounds` at object offsets 0x3C..0x3F. Ordinary attack/object overlap instead first tests the fixed damage rectangle at 0x38..0x3B and, when available, the sprite rectangle at 0x34..0x37. A large visible or damage effect is therefore not evidence for a large Bomb Block scan.

See [attack tile-scan dispatch](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/asm/code_08032E98.s#L8630-L8636), [rectangle extraction and quantization](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/asm/code_08032E98.s#L6946-L7072), [tile clipping and iteration](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/asm/code_08032E98.s#L7073-L7237), [damage/sprite overlap and callbacks](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/asm/code_08032E98.s#L7674-L7960), and [fixed box vs ObjectSetBounds setters](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/kirby.c#L5823-L5837).

For a right-facing attack at fixed-point center Qx,Qy, let px=floor(Qx/256), py=floor(Qy/256), with integer local scan rectangle (l,t,r,b). The scanned tile columns are **floor((px+l)/16) through floor((px+r)/16), inclusive**; rows are analogous. The scanner clips to levelMinPosition/levelMaxPosition. Left-facing bounds are mirrored first. Apply this formula at each actual registered movement, not to a filled union rectangle. The Bomb Block's center can consequently lie beyond the numerical edge while its tile overlaps the scan. Report tile coordinates and Kirby subpixel position for an exact boundary witness.

Ordinary Bomb Block collision byte 0xF1 has attributes 0x00101260. The scanner requires attribute 0x1000, then for attribute 0x20 clears the tile and starts `sub_0800E0E4`, whose later updates propagate through neighboring tiles with attribute 0x40. Intervening ordinary solids do not stop the rectangle scan. Tile 0xF6 is different: attributes 0x00301220 require attack-category bit 0x4000 before its chain can start. Do not transfer ordinary 0xF1 conclusions to that restricted trigger. See [collision attribute table](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_080023A4.c#L71-L104), [tile-category filters and chain creation](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/asm/code_08032E98.s#L7238-L7333), and [chain propagation](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0800E0E4.c#L11-L153).

For object-kind 2 attacks versus object-kind 1 switches, the ROM callback table at 0x0834BD88 contains callbacks 0x080364E5, 0x080365C9, and 0x0803699D; the latter two are used in the two attack/object dispatch directions. The small switch constructor adds flags 0x02608000 to its base flags and sets resistance mask 0x00308020. InitObject starts this type with low-three-bit resistance zero, so strength 3 passes. Its current flags/state can still suppress a later interaction. Its relevant contact branch uses `(attack.unk68 & ~switch.unk5C & 0x003FFFF8) != 0` plus low-three-bit strength comparison before setting the switch's 0x40000 interaction flag. Its 0x8000 damage immunity does not itself prevent that interaction flag. Beam/Spark category 0x400, Fighter/Parasol/kicked Ice 0x40, Fire 0x100, Ice breath 0x200, Throw 0x1000, Master 0x7100, and Crash 0x10000 have an allowed category; Magic/Cook's 0x8000 (and Magic's optional 0x100000) are masked out. This establishes a compatible source contact path, **not a complete route or every switch-template branch**. Switch template flags and its 0xB6 state select interaction-flag versus body/contact logic.

See [small-switch creation](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/asm/big_small_switch.s#L47-L101), [normal attack interaction-mask branch](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/asm/code_08032E98.s#L6086-L6187), [small-switch interaction selector](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/asm/big_small_switch.s#L564-L610), and [callback dispatch](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/asm/code_08032E98.s#L7930-L7960).

## Shared coordinate, collision, and despawn rules

Positions and velocities use 8 fractional bits: `x += xspeed`, `y -= yspeed`. The common projectile update first runs cancellation/despawn and callbacks, then moves, then queries terrain only if flag `0x100` is absent, then registers/displays the attack. See [`sub_08070580`, code_0806F780.c:1110–1133](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L1110-L1133). The terrain helper itself also checks `0x100`: [`sub_0806FC70`:788–835](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L788-L835).

For the ordinary projectile pre-update path, the projectile origin must lie within at least one matching-room camera's rectangle, using integer-truncated origin coordinates: camera-left −48 through camera-left +288; camera-top −48 through camera-top +208. This is a 48-pixel margin around the 240×160 viewport. See [`ObjectPreUpdate`:724–741](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L724-L741) and [`sub_0803D80C`, kirby.c:5502–5531](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/kirby.c#L5502-L5531). Culling occurs before the next movement, so the final registered attack can extend one movement step plus its hitbox beyond that rectangle. Room/ability changes and other cancellations also apply.

Consequently, “no intrinsic timer” does not mean an attack can hit infinitely far away. Nor is its cap a universal number of pixels from launch: that number depends on the camera, available matching-room viewpoints, starting position, and flight direction. For a fixed camera and a right-moving constant-speed shot with valid starting center X0, camera-left L, and step v, its exact last movement count in fixed-point units is `floor((256*(L+288)+255-Q0)/V)+1`, where Q0 is the starting fixed-point center and V is the positive fixed-point velocity, before other cancellations. For integer-valued starts/steps this agrees with the simpler `floor((L+288-X0)/v)+1`; retain the +255 term for fractional starts/steps. Add the appropriate damage edge or tile-scan edge to the resulting center, then apply the target-specific integer/tile rule. Integer/subpixel positions need to be preserved when applying this formula. Example only: with camera-left 0 and stationary integer Kirby x=120, the UFO shot starts at 130, last moves to 292, and reaches damage edge 302, or 182 px from Kirby. This is not a universal tested UFO maximum.

The fixed hitbox setter stores a left/top offset and half-extents: [`sub_0803E2B0`, kirby.c:5823–5828](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/kirby.c#L5823-L5828). Facing-left boxes are mirrored by collision code; representative integer-coordinate comparisons are in [`asm/code_08032E98.s:11800–11925`](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/asm/code_08032E98.s#L11800-L11925). Do not confuse the separate `ObjectSetBounds` rectangle with the fixed damage box or animation-driven hitbox.

## Spark versus Beam

**Bomb Block correction:** Spark scans x ±26, y −22..30; Beam scans only ±2 around each moving center, so its forward tile-scan envelope is 55 px, while its damage envelope remains 59 px. Spark is not range-equivalent in either path.

Spark's creator sets the damage box to `(−23, −20, +23, +26)` and the separate bounds to `(−26, −22, +26, +30)`: [`sub_08074AB0`:2780–2808](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L2780-L2808). Its update copies Kirby's position every tick, requires animation `0x34`, and registers the attack directly; it performs no terrain collision query: [`sub_08074C1C`:2811–2881](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L2811-L2881). The attack state can continue while held: [`sub_0805D630`, kirby.c:15764–15784](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/kirby.c#L15764-L15784).

Beam's damaging projectile has a ±6 box, flag `0x100`, and no collision-termination callbacks. Its six speed pairs are in [`gUnk_08350D7E`:481–488](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L481-L488); creation and update are [`sub_0807CFE4`/`sub_0807D208`:5448–5513](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L5448-L5513). Each segment resets its origin to Kirby x ±13, y+2 and moves five times. Horizontal segment speed `0x800` gives a center reach of 53 px, damage edge of 59 px, and tile-scan edge of 55 px. The outer damage-box vertical envelope is approximately 41 px above and 36 px below an integer Kirby origin, but not at every horizontal distance.

Tick count was checked against [`Macro_080FC150`, object.h:293–332](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/include/object.h#L293-L332) and the decoded ROM animation `0x103` at `0x083F51D0`. The header advances one per update at normal animation speed, the callback resets at five, and counter >5 deletes before a seventh segment moves. Beam can be cancelled when Kirby leaves animations `0x34`/`0x35` or loses Beam: [`sub_08084F30`:8513–8523](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L8513-L8523).

Source conclusion: Spark and Beam both avoid ordinary terrain obstruction, but Spark is not range-equivalent to Beam. Source alone does not establish that Spark solves every specific Beam gate, even when a gate lies nearby. The actual target tile or hitbox, approach position, target eligibility, and vertical alignment must be tested.

## UFO top-charge piercing shot

Its Bomb Block scan box is ±6 horizontally and ±4 vertically, smaller than its fixed damage box. The example camera limit in the shared section gives tile-scan edge 298 rather than damage edge 302.

[`sub_0807C6C0`:5199–5241](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L5199-L5241) sets speed ±`0x600`, spawn x ±`0xA00`, y+`0x200`, fixed box `(−16, −14, +10, +14)`, flag `0x100`, and null per-tick/object-hit/terrain-hit callbacks. It therefore travels horizontally through ordinary terrain until camera/room/cancellation limits apply. There is no per-shot distance counter in this path. Ability loss or animation `0x5A` invalidates it through [`sub_08084EDC`:8497–8510](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L8497-L8510).

Do not generalize this to the medium shot: [`sub_0807C48C`:5155–5196](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L5155-L5196) has terrain and object-hit callbacks and does not set `0x100`.

## Master charged wave

Its Bomb Block scan uses local (−8,−12,8,12): same 118 px forward maximum as damage, but only y−20..4 relative to launch Kirby.

Creation: [`sub_08082B5C`:7613–7646](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L7613-L7646). Spawn is x ±16, y−8; fixed damage box `(−10, −20, +8, +20)` in right-facing coordinates, initial horizontal speed 6.5 px/tick. Update: [`sub_08082CCC`:7649–7675](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L7649-L7675).

Counter starts at zero. Counter values 0–11 move at 6.5, counter 12 switches speed to 4 before moving, and counters 12–15 move at 4. Counter 16 invalidates the room before `ObjectPreUpdate`, so there is no seventeenth movement. Maximum center displacement from spawn = `12×6.5 + 4×4 = 94`; forward damage edge and tile-scan edge = `16 + 94 + 8 = 118`. The damage box relative to launch Kirby is y −28..+12; the tile-scan box is y −20..+4. The custom update contains no terrain query. Master loss, cancellation animation, or camera despawn can shorten flight. Callsite identifying the charged wave is [`kirby.c:17166–17232`](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/kirby.c#L17166-L17232).

## Sword / Smash / Master shared landing shockwave

The update copies the active sprite hitbox to ObjectSetBounds. Therefore the Bomb Block scan shares the derived 85.875 px coordinate envelope, subject to integer/tile quantization.

Smash enters the shared rising/landing sword state via [`kirby.c:21193–21201`](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/kirby.c#L21193-L21201); the landing state creates the shockwave in [`kirby.c:17474–17507`](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/kirby.c#L17474-L17507).

[`sub_08080004`:6590–6618](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L6590-L6618) creates it at Kirby's origin with speed `0x620` = 6.125 and flags `0xA0000100`, explicitly including terrain-bypass bit `0x100`. [`sub_08080130`:6621–6656](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L6621-L6656) has no terrain query. It increments the counter before checking >9, so exactly nine movement ticks occur. Ticks 1–7 use 6.125; tick 8 changes animation variant and speed to 3.5 before movement; ticks 8–9 use 3.5. Center displacement = `7×6.125 + 2×3.5 = 49.875`.

The hitbox is animation-driven, not a constant ±box. ROM table root `0x083B909C` points to animation table `0x08448574`. Animation `0x9C` variant 0 starts at `0x083EAD4C`, variant 1 at `0x083EADDC`. Decoded `−6` hitbox commands:

| Record | Local box left, top, right, bottom | Applied movement ticks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `0x083EAD64` | 0, −19, 38, 9 | 1–3 |
| `0x083EAD84` | 0, −19, 34, 10 | 4–6 |
| `0x083EADA4` | −1, −20, 35, 11 | 7 |
| `0x083EADF4` (variant 1) | 5, −18, 36, 10 | 8–9 |

Other later records exist but are not reached before this object's timer ends. The shared damage/tile-scan leading-edge sequence is 44.125, 50.25, 56.375, 58.5, 64.625, 70.75, 77.875, 82.375, 85.875 px. Thus adding the largest historical box edge 38 to the final center would overstate range; the active final edge is 36. Decode semantics and animation timing are in [`sprite.c:759–795`](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/sprite.c#L759-L795) and [`sub_08155494`:902–921](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/sprite.c#L902-L921). The 85.875 number is a coordinate envelope, not a claim that an arbitrary point target 86 pixels away is hit in both directions. This shared shockwave also permits Sword and Master, per the ability test at lines 6627–6628.

## Ice frozen enemies

**Target restriction:** this object never enables the 0x80000000 tile-scan flag. Kicking enables 0x20000000 object attacks and clears 0x50040000; the ordinary terrain query remains bypassed. Thus it has a compatible small-switch object-contact path, but **no direct Bomb Block-breaking scan**. Its unlimited-by-timer travel must not be described as an unlimited Bomb Block range. Ice breath below is a different effect.

Creation: [`sub_0807DBCC`:5764–5832](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L5764-L5832). Frozen-object flags include `0x100`. Damage box is ±5 for a small tile-count enemy, ±11 for the ordinary/specified special cases, or ±16 for a large sprite. These are enemy-dependent, not interchangeable ice blocks.

[`sub_0807DE28`:5835–5894](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L5835-L5894) follows the original enemy until that parent is removed, then enables the idle frozen state. The idle expiry condition is `0x10000000` and `++counter > 0x78`, i.e. it expires on the 121st counted idle update if not kicked. [`sub_0807E098`:5897–5915](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L5897-L5915) launches at ±4 px/tick away from the contacting Kirby and clears `0x50040000`, including the idle-expiry bit. It does not install a replacement flight timer. The common update skips terrain because `0x100` remains set.

The launched object's hit callback [`sub_08084C34`:8384–8394](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L8384-L8394) ignores other frozen objects (animation `0x28B`) and `OBJ_VERTICAL_SLIDING_DOOR` (type `0x71`); other object hits cause a shatter effect and deletion. “Passes terrain” therefore does not imply it survives every intervening object. The practical distance begins at the frozen enemy's center, not necessarily Kirby's center.

## Laser and Cupid: remote targets, not terrain bypass

Both use separate ±1 tile-scan bounds. The larger damage boxes below must not be used as exact Bomb Block scan dimensions.

Laser: [`sub_0807BCE0`:4949–4991](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L4949-L4991) sets ±8 px/tick, spawn 2 px behind Kirby, ±6×±3 damage box, and no `0x100`. Its terrain callback [`sub_0807C1A0`:5046–5152](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L5046-L5152) redirects horizontal/vertical flight at compatible slope orientations, stops on ordinary solid collision, and deletes when the accepted-redirect counter becomes >3. Object hits delete it through [`sub_08084BD8`:8366–8369](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L8366-L8369). The source supports open-line or slope-routed reach, not arbitrary aiming or wall penetration. It has the common camera limit and ability/cancellation guard, not a fixed flight lifetime.

Cupid: [`sub_08081864`:7049–7096](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L7049-L7096) spawns 6 px in front, with local damage box `(−6, −3, 0, +3)`. Charge selects a speed parameter from 4.5 through 7 px/tick using [`gUnk_08350C88`:356–367](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L356-L367). Up/neutral/down input selects three distinct initial velocity formulas, not free directional aiming. At maximum charge their source-derived initial fixed-point (vx, vy) values are respectively (994,1245), (1757,322), and (1267,−1274), before facing reflection and the first gravity/drag update. These are approximately (3.883,4.863), (6.863,1.258), and (4.949,−4.977) px/tick, with positive vy upward. Higher charge can also fire three arrows with different charge values: [`kirby.c:20679–20716`](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/kirby.c#L20679-L20716).

In unobstructed air, before each movement, absolute horizontal speed decreases by 6/256 down to a floor of 0.5 px/tick, and upward velocity decreases by 24/256 down to −4.5 px/tick. Water uses different gravity and floors. Exact recurrence and move order: [`sub_08081EB0`:7273–7318](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L7273-L7318). The arrow queries terrain and stops/embeds on collision, with separate object-hit behavior: [7185–7272](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L7185-L7272). Its embedded-arrow timeout at 200 is not an active-flight range timer. A same-height range calculation would still be conditional on charge, aim, camera retention, target size, and a clear ballistic path; this report deliberately does not present one as a universal maximum.

## Additional terrain-overlap effects and omissions

### Fighter: distinguish the 1-HP wave from the normal waves

The 1-HP projectile explicitly sets 0xA0000100, has null object-hit/terrain-hit callbacks, starts 8 px forward at 4.125 px/tick, and uses tile bounds ±10 with damage (−12,−14,10,14). Its pre-movement callback increments the timer and deletes at>20, giving 20 movements: **8+20×4.125+10=100.5 px**. Kirby's release function chooses it only at hp==1. The ordinary small and charged waves lack 0x100: the small one stops on terrain; the larger one tolerates destructible-tile attribute 0x1000 but is destroyed by other terrain. Their open-air forward damage bounds are 76.5 and 91 px respectively, not general wall-passing maxima. See [1-HP creator](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L6250-L6290), [20-update callback](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L8419-L8426), [hp branch](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/kirby.c#L19519-L19544), [ordinary wave creators](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L6164-L6247), [ordinary terrain callbacks](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L8396-L8417).

Fighter also has attached, animation-driven effects with no terrain query. The first combo (animation 0x1FA,variant 0) contains an active box (0,−19,60,11), copied into tile bounds: **60 px forward** without the 1-HP condition. Variants 1 and 2 reach 39 and 37 px forward; the separate 0x1FF effect reaches 44 px. These are short-lived melee effects tied to the appropriate Kirby state, not traveling projectile distances. The 60 px box is repeated at ROM records 0x0840E3EC..0x0840E520. See [Fighter combo creator/update](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L6023-L6105), [other combo creators](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L6108-L6163), [separate melee effect](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L6448-L6516).

### Parasol

`sub_08077574` follows Kirby and performs no terrain query. It copies active sprite bounds to ObjectSetBounds, but enables the 0x80000000 tile-scan flag only for Kirby animation 0x35 or 0x42. Both use the 9-frame forward-swing sequence (0xBF/0 or 0xC0/0). The last active frame has (16,−11,48,11): **48 px forward** for both object overlap and tile scanning. Earlier backswing reaches 32 px behind; there are zero-box gaps, so this is not a persistent filled area. ROM command records 0x083EEFE4 /0x083EF3F4 verify the final box. See [creator and state/bounds update](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L3440-L3586), [animation mapping](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/kirby.c#L4841-L4949).

### Ice breath and Fire breath

These use the same custom no-terrain-query update. They are not the kicked frozen enemy. Each breath packet starts 24 px forward, with fixed damage (−10,−12,10,16), tile bounds ±3, initial vx 0x108, and horizontal acceleration `0x38-(Rand16()&31)`, i.e.25..56 fixed-point units per update. Before each movement the acceleration is added to velocity. At the end of its animation cycle the packet is reanchored to Kirby and its speed reset. Both animation 0x4D (Fire, ROM 0x083E21FC) and 0xD7 (Ice,0x083F14E8) have delays 3,3,3,1,2,1 then end. Including the registered movement that reaches the end command gives **14 movements per normal cycle**. With N=14, forward scan edge=`24+N*v0/256+a*N*(N+1)/(2*256)+3`.

Ice does not use Fire's directional speed adjustment, giving v0=264 and a≤56, hence **64.40625 px scan /71.40625 px damage**. Fire may increase idleTimer to 0x120 while pressing forward; a subsequent cycle uses v0=0x108+0x120=552, giving **80.15625 px scan /87.15625 px damage**. These are source upper envelopes over RNG and held-input state, not demonstrated tight maxima or guarantees that every RNG trajectory reaches them. Down/up aiming and vertical random acceleration change the arc. For Ice, vertical scan displacement is bounded by−15.71484375..3 relative to the cycle's Kirby origin; for fully aimed Fire the coordinate-wise bounds are−29.71484375..13.5. Coordinate extrema need not occur at the same time or RNG sequence.

The additional small attached tile-only effect has bounds (0,−4,16,4); it does not extend the forward maxima above. See [Fire creator/update](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L2319-L2423), [Ice creator](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L5632-L5689), [Fire input caps](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/kirby.c#L15617-L15730), [tile-only near-mouth effect](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L2425-L2465), [animation timing](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/sprite.c#L759-L795).

### Throw: a tile-breaking halo, not an unrestricted piercing throw

The held payload uses 0x100, but launch `sub_0807B200` explicitly clears it and invokes terrain physics. Horizontal speed is 4 px/tick; angled speeds are (±916,+/−458)/256. The active payload damage square is ±11 ordinary/±16 large. A separate following object `sub_0807BAE0` has flags 0x80000500 and tile bounds ±10 ordinary/±20 large, but no damage box. It scans tiles without a terrain query while the payload remains active; as soon as the payload rebounds and sets 0x200, the halo deletes. This can provide **local scan reach beyond the payload's terrain-contact edge**, but no general wall-crossing flight range follows. Holding/aiming offsets, collision resolution and the payload's chosen size are essential for any target-specific maximum. See [held payload](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L4492-L4590), [release and terrain physics](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L4764-L4848), [rebound/halo](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L4851-L4946).

### Bomb and Missile blasts

Bomb's traveling object uses terrain physics. Its explosion has 0xA0000100, a fixed ±16 tile scan and a damage box that grows 14→16→20 px before disabling at counter 12. Missile's explosion has a fixed ±24 tile scan and damage that grows to ±30 before counter 8 disables it. Both are local area effects without a line-of-sight query. Their radii are relative to the detonation center, not automatically to the position where Kirby launched or started moving. See [Missile and Bomb explosion creators](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L2552-L2620), [blast updates](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L2674-L2732), [Bomb flight terrain](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L7478-L7538).

### Crash, Magic, Cook

Crash's screen object has camera-centered damage bounds ±120×±80, no terrain query, and **no 0x80000000 tile-scan flag**. Its category 0x10000 passes the small-switch interaction mask; subject to the switch's activation branch and overlap it can therefore contact a switch without a clear line of sight. This is a 240×160 viewport effect, not a whole-room range. Its update can process hits across repeated registrations.

Cook's capture and Magic's item-conversion screen object also use 240×160 camera-centered rectangles but no tile-scan flag. Their 0x8000 category is masked by the small switch; Magic's optional 0x100000 is masked too. Consequently these screen effects should not be granted as generic Bomb Block or small-switch tools. Cook registers its capture once; Magic registers five times. This does not classify Magic's separate invincibility or Meta Knight outcomes as the same effect. See [Crash](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L7678-L7745), [Magic](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L7748-L7790), [Cook](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L5976-L6020).

### Other local overlap cases, not traveling-wall grants

- **UFO short Beam whip:** `sub_0807C954`/`sub_0807CB4C`,8×5-tick rays from x ±10,y+4, with maximum horizontal speed 8. Thus 52 px tile-scan /56 px damage edge. The other visual whip objects at 0x196/0x197 are flagged 0x300 (disabled) and are not extra damaging reach. See [whip speed table](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L448-L457), [whip creator/update](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L5263-L5330), [disabled visual objects](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L5332-L5446).
- **Sword/Hammer/Master melee and Smash melee:** the attached weapon creators set 0x100 and their updates copy animation boxes to ObjectSetBounds without terrain queries. This is genuine local overlap beyond a wall boundary, but a different attack-state catalog from the traveling waves. Sword's decoded table includes a 58 px forward combo frame; Hammer's includes 33 px forward. The source-backed wave limits in the main table remain larger for Sword/Master/Smash. Master has additional state-dependent weapon offsets, so no exact maximum over every Master melee state is asserted here. See [Sword](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L2884-L3064), [Master](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L3066-L3283), [Hammer](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L3285-L3438), [Smash weapon effects](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L6660-L6991).
- **Cutter:** initial 0x100 is temporary. The update clears it when counter==1; subsequent motion uses terrain collision and rebound behavior. Do not call all Cutter flight terrain-piercing. See [Cutter creation/flight](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L3904-L4095).
- **Body-attached effects:** Burning uses tile bounds (−12,−10,12,7); Stone uses (−6,−6,6,11), with a wider damage box during some frames; Wheel uses (−6,−6,14,12); Smash's Stone-like effect uses ±18 tile bounds and ±22 damage. These effects follow Kirby and do not independently query terrain, but Kirby's own collision still limits how close their origin can approach a wall. Tornado's attached damage effect has no tile-scan flag in this path. This does not prove an entire ability cannot break a tile through another contact path. See [Burning and Stone](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L4098-L4212), [Tornado and Wheel](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L4360-L4488), [Smash local effect](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L6993-L7047).
- **Normal underwater spray:** not a Copy Ability, but the broad wording also makes it worth retaining as an alternative. `sub_08079D64` has no terrain query, updates both damage and tile rectangles, and resets its trajectory every 8 updates. For stationary Kirby its horizontal source envelope is 35.5 px facing right or 37.5 px left (different spawn offsets); upward/downward spray reaches 39.5 px vertically. This is a short oscillating packet attached to the underwater attack state, not a persistent projectile. See [water spray creator/update](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/code_0806F780.c#L4214-L4358), [water attack entry](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam/blob/7d969fbce14fdc838d2c1ea01389717fb96c3189/src/kirby.c#L14064-L14085).

This extension identifies concrete omissions and rejects several misleading flag-only classifications. It is not a verified catalog of every possible damage state, held-enemy size/offset, multiplayer interaction or geometry exploit. Route-level grants remain limited to complete witnesses. No emulator was run for this source audit.

## Use with runtime evidence

For each proposed gate replacement, record the exact room/target, approach side and coordinate, launch height, attack variant, target hitbox distance, and an actual successful/failed activation. Spark opening a different upper Bomb Block must not be presented as proof about a lower gate. These source results explain why Spark has a shorter local reach, why launched frozen enemies can cross terrain but do not directly scan Bomb Blocks, and why some projectiles require camera-aware rather than timer-only range tests. They do not establish that all existing Beam classifications should also accept Spark or Ice.

### Bounded Normal family witness: Olive 815

`olive-normal815-source-witness.md` records sustained inhale moving, not
consuming/spitting, silver block `0x088CCCFC` into maintained switch contact.
Intrinsic Normal air puff then triggers the upper switch for a timed lower-gate
crossing and ordinary slide/chest continuation. Actual 1/6 HP × 100/500% runs

wall-piercing nor generic Normal/block/puff equivalence; the matched final-puff

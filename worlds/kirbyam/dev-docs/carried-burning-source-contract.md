# Carried Burning alternatives for native 141 and 742

This checkpoint adds source-backed alternatives to the three native 141 target
rows and the two clean native 742 target rows, based on published `754bac4`.
It removes their conditional fallback without changing AP topology, source
assignments, starting items or options. The native 141 reverse row also has a
lower-destination bypass; that alternative does not cross metal in reverse.

## Representation and source policy

Graph alternatives belong inside the existing target rows. The installer ORs those
graphs and preserves the original AP rule. Additional rows on one entrance would
instead AND the requirements and are not appropriate.

Reuse `ordinary_hub_statues` unchanged: all 30 physical placements, actual source
assignments, directed hub guards and existing documented statue-retouch fairness.
Do not prune other possible abilities or treat a finite mixed enemy grant as
renewable. Every new graph starts Normal at an independently reachable source
region. Permission ownership cannot substitute for acquisition or physical carry.

The existing local Flamer witnesses remain compiled identically. New carrier parts
contain no source, ability recovery, persistent opening or room-reset state. The
new alternatives do not turn a local Flamer into a renewable provider. The only
new capability class is `ordinary_block_hit`, with Burning as its sole
reviewed provider. It uses the existing attack-provider machinery, introduces no
new search state or equipment effect, and rejects cross-room or persistent-open
use. Swimming maps Burning to Normal Water Gun, so it cannot satisfy this dry
ordinary-block action. No persistent evaluation cache is added.

## Applicability and validation

The three native 141 rows and two clean native 742 rows are unconditional: their
row-level `requires_guaranteed_source` conditions are removed. Otherwise, the old
Flamer condition would suppress the new alternatives precisely when needed.

An unconditional row means every policy must find at least one accepted graph;
it does not assert every source assignment works. Unsupported assignments remain
closed, existing bounded whole-policy feasibility may resample shuffled policies,
and the final all-check guard rejects actual self-locks. Do not silently alter
source assignments, user options, permission classification or starting items.

Validation requirements for this checkpoint:

1. Exact compiled and full-evaluator equivalence of unchanged local witnesses,
   existing 707 graphs and shared hub graph across both Area Key settings.
2. Source/permission/access negatives: absent or behind-target source, wrong
   assignment, mixed finite grant, consumed Flamer, Normal intermediate starts,
   missing connection/Area Key, and merely reaching native 712 or a dirty return.
3. One target row per AP target, OR alternatives, preserved base rule, and correct
   indirect source-region dependencies for entrances. Test actual unlock/connection
   items placed behind the gated targets to preserve self-lock rejection.
4. Native evidence integrity, scaler identity, frame continuity, exact source
   substitutions, actual door/endpoints, metal/Bomb changes, default/500 lineage,
   and the explicit Normal postcondition of the joint 742 continuation.
5. Full source-expansion reference equivalence and paired state/queue benchmarks,
   preserving source outcomes and existing cache boundaries. Reusing parts should
   increase witnesses from 92 to 102 without adding target rows.
6. Generation option matrix, all-check guard, coverage catalog, complete relevant
   room topology, location IDs, RoomSanity and slot snapshots. Classify any changed
   sample as accepted or safely rejected with exact dependency evidence, not by
   weakening feasibility or adding a permissive fallback.

This checkpoint does not certify physical native 141 reverse beyond its old local
witness, native 742 upper-return, native 708 joint-return uncertainty, Ice/native 825, or the remaining boss-source gaps.
Starting Burning and Stone remain unchanged until the separate release audit is
complete. Native no-loss/source arguments are not direct AP 1 HP replay or a
universal claim about all intermediate enemy-health multipliers.

## Exact minimal-fill regression

The unchanged seed 271418054 and source policy now produce six different item
placements. These five rows previously skipped their traversal rules when Flamer
was not guaranteed; their unconditional source-aware rules correctly close that
unsupported fallback. The actual new fill has 57 progression-stranded checks
(previously 44), while every check remains reachable with all inventory. The
production all-check guard rejects the seed and produces no archive.

The regression preserves the historical 754bac4 expected list and six old
placements. Replaying those placements under the new rules also remains rejected
(295 progression-stranded checks), with no inventory-infeasible checks. This is a
counterfactual safety check, not a generated seed or a claim that adding physical
routes alone reduces reachability. No guard, retry budget, starting inventory, or
accessibility option was weakened to make the test pass.
